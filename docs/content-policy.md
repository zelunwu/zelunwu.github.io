# Public-content policy

The website is a reviewed public projection, not a mirror of a private career repository.

## Allowed by default

- Name and public professional headline
- Public GitHub and ORCID profiles
- User-confirmed education
- Organizations and roles explicitly marked public
- Published papers with public bibliographic metadata; preserve formal English titles and DOI destinations across locales
- Public product contributions explicitly approved for CV/public use
- General research themes that do not expose internal implementations

## Prohibited without explicit review

- Private email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, and financial information
- Raw evidence, screenshots, source documents, and private-repository links
- Internal project names, organization units, milestones, issue numbers, and roadmaps
- Performance, quality, coverage, customer, revenue, or team-size metrics
- Unverified job titles, responsibilities, dates, or impact claims
- Sanitized internal project summaries that have not separately been approved for publication

Every content pull request must confirm that new public facts satisfy this policy.

Localized copy may clarify public facts but must not strengthen titles, responsibilities, contribution claims, or impact. Formal publication titles remain unchanged unless the publisher supplies an official translation.
